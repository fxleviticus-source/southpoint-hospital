About South Point Hospitals

A private healthcare network serving Lusaka with 24/7 support across multiple branches.

Who We Are

A healthcare network built for Lusaka

South Point Hospitals is a private healthcare network operating multiple branches across Lusaka, Zambia. The network provides a broad range of medical and wellness services, with 24/7 healthcare support available for emergency and outpatient needs.

South Point Hospitals serves different healthcare needs, including general care, specialized clinics, maternal and child health, wellness services, diagnostics, and emergency care.

What We Provide

A broad range of healthcare services

General Care

24-hour outpatient care, pharmacy, laboratory and diagnostic services.

Specialized Clinics

Cardiac, surgical, orthopedic, physiotherapy, ENT and skin clinics.

Maternal & Child Health

GYNE & OBS, antenatal, maternity, pediatric and under-5 vaccination services.

Emergency Care

24-hour emergency medical, ambulance and critical care services.

24/7 Availability

Healthcare support around the clock

South Point Hospitals offers 24/7 healthcare support, including outpatient care, pharmacy, laboratory, X-ray and diagnostics, and emergency and ambulance services — available whenever they're needed across our Lusaka branches.
